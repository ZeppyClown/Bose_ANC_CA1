# Final presentation review — 15 September 2026

## Overall assessment

The current website substantively aligns with the CA1 content requirements. It explains commercialisation, includes all four CIM areas, and makes entrepreneurship the mechanism connecting decisions across them. The strongest improvement is the persistent CIM diagram with Assemble, Redirect and Respond. No content redesign or extra chapter is needed before rehearsal.

This is a content and browser review, not an awarded mark. The website alone cannot establish compliance with the two delivery criteria or the required PowerPoint/MP4 submission.

## Product, Market and Entrepreneurship overlap

The remaining overlap is purposeful if each speaker owns a different question:

- Product: what had to change in the design?
- Market: who would buy it, and how would they experience the benefit?
- Entrepreneurship: who used information from one area to change a decision in another?

TriPort, the early cost objection and airline response can be recalled in F. They should not become a second detailed timeline. Avoid repeating D's battery comparison in E or F.

## Remaining priorities, in order

1. **One combined timed rehearsal.** The 8:35 target is a budget, not an observed duration. With 35 visual states, pauses and handoffs must be timed too.
2. **Keep the entrepreneur visible in the explanation.** Name Amar Bose, the complementary engineers and the marketing signal; then describe the decision and its consequence.
3. **Protect the commercial evidence boundary.** Product purchase evidence is useful; the 13% is brand-wide online headphone dollar share in a defined 2015 panel. The presentation does not establish profit or a complete causal explanation of sales.
4. **Check the actual submission artifacts.** PowerPoint plus MP4, maximum 10 minutes, MP4 under 500 MB; every member visible and speaking; consistent audio. The website and its print function do not complete these requirements.

No further decorative animation or extra slides are recommended before these checks.
